Our process

From first call to a live website in five steps

A clear, simple process so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

1

Discovery call

We learn about your business, customers, competitors and goals, then agree on scope, price and timeline in writing.

2

Planning and design

We plan pages and structure, then share designs for your review before any code is written.

3

Development

We build the site with clean, fast code, test it on phones and laptops, and fix issues before you see it.

4

SEO and content setup

Meta data, schema, sitemap, speed work and Google Business Profile setup so the site is ready to be found.

5

Launch and support

We take your site live, hand over access and stay available on WhatsApp for questions and updates.
